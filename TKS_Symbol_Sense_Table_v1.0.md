## Element Structure

Each Element is a Noetic (0-9) expressing through a World (A-D):

| World | Letter | Domain | Quality |
|-------|--------|--------|---------|
| Spiritual (Atziluth) | A | Divine blueprints, purpose, soul | Slowest to change, deepest impact |
| Mental (Briah) | B | Thought, ideas, concepts | Changes fast, reverts fast |
| Emotional (Yetzirah) | C | Feelings, desires, energy | Volatile, contagious |
| Physical (Assiyah) | D | Matter, action, manifestation | Stubborn, permanent once changed |

| Noetic | Number | Core Meaning |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| IDEA | 0 | Pure potential, all possibilities, specific concept |
| MIND | 1 | Consciousness, awareness, observer |
| POSITIVE | 2 | Magnetic attraction, order, what draws attention |
| NEGATIVE | 3 | Rejection, repulsion, disorder, force field |
| VIBRATION | 4 | Intensity, volume, energy level |
| FEMALE | 5 | Receptivity, holding, accumulated beliefs, vessel |
| MALE | 6 | Active principle, delivery, structure, containment |
| RHYTHM | 7 | Repetition, cycles, patterns, habits |
| ABOVE/CAUSE | 8 | Triggers, causes, elevation, authority |
| BELOW/EFFECT | 9 | Responses, effects, grounding, foundation |
