# APPENDIX C: Glossary of Terms

**Above/Cause (Noetic 8)**: An Idea that triggers a different Idea to come to Mind. The cause in a cause-effect relationship.

**ACBE**: Diagnostic protocol for tracing effects to their causes. Above-Cause/Below-Effect analysis.

**Assiah**: The fourth and lowest world; the physical/material plane. Represented by letter D.

**Atziluth**: The first and highest world; the spiritual/divine plane. Represented by letter A.

**Beast**: The operational construct consisting of Mark, Name, and Number that anchors a consciousness operation.

**Below/Effect (Noetic 9)**: The Idea brought to Mind by an Above/Cause trigger. The effect in a cause-effect relationship.

**Briah**: The second world; the mental/intellectual plane. Represented by letter B.

**Confirmation**: Signs from reality that an operation has been received and is processing. Comes in three waves.

**Doctrine**: The ideas and beliefs that organize perception and behavior. One of the Three Controls.

**Element**: A Noetic expressed at a specific World-level. 40 total (10 Noetics × 4 Worlds).

**Female (Noetic 5)**: A Mind impregnated with Ideas; the Ideas that compose a Mind. Receptivity and conditioning.

**FM Protocol**: Female-Male Authority protocol for obtaining permission from relevant authorities for operations.

**Foundation**: One of seven fundamental life areas: Unity, Wisdom, Life, Companionship, Power, Material, Lust.

**Gnosis**: Altered state of consciousness required for effective operations. Achieved through various methods.

**Hierarchy**: The structure of authority relationships. One of the Three Controls.

**Idea (Noetic 10)**: All things in and outside the Mind. The universal content that consciousness processes.

**Male (Noetic 6)**: An Idea impregnated with other Ideas; the internal composition of an Idea. Structure and content.

**Mark**: The visual symbol component of a Beast. Drawn to anchor the operation.

**Mind (Noetic 1)**: Various degrees of awareness that process, store, output, and input Ideas.

**MVR Protocol**: Mind-Vibration-Rhythm protocol for installing new programming in consciousness.

**Name**: The linguistic component of a Beast. Derived from the desire statement.

**Negative (Noetic 3)**: An Idea the Mind is averse to and repelled by. Rejection and avoidance.

**Noetic**: One of ten fundamental operations of consciousness as Mind and Idea interact.

**Noetic Fractal**: The nesting of Noetics within Noetics (e.g., 5.1.3 = Negative within Mind within Female).

**Number**: The numerical component of a Beast. Serves as tracking identifier.

**Operation**: A formal consciousness procedure designed to produce specific manifestation.

**Positive (Noetic 2)**: An Idea the Mind has affinity for and is attracted toward. Desire and attraction.

**Prerequisite**: A required foundation for manifestation. The three prerequisites are Desire, Power, and Wisdom.

**Resources**: Material necessities and assets. One of the Three Controls.

**Rhythm (Noetic 7)**: The rate and pattern a Mind is exposed to an Idea. Timing and repetition.

**RPM**: Reality-Program-Manifestation analysis. Diagnostic framework for operations.

**TKS**: TOOTRA Kabalistic System. The operational framework described in this text.

**TOOTRA**: The source teaching from which TKS derives.

**Vibration (Noetic 4)**: The degree to which an Idea resonates with Mind. Frequency and impression strength.

**World**: One of four planes of existence (Atziluth, Briah, Yetzirah, Assiah) corresponding to spiritual, mental, emotional, and physical levels.

**Yetzirah**: The third world; the emotional/astral plane. Represented by letter C.

# APPENDIX D: Safety Checklist

## Pre-Operation Safety Check

**Physical Safety:**
- [ ] Environment is private and secure
- [ ] No intoxicants in system
- [ ] Basic physical needs met (rested, fed, hydrated)
- [ ] No driving or dangerous activities planned immediately after

**Mental Safety:**
- [ ] Not in acute mental health crisis
- [ ] Emotionally stable enough to handle intensity
- [ ] Clear intention established
- [ ] Not operating from desperation or rage

**Ethical Safety:**
- [ ] Operation does not target specific person's will
- [ ] Operation does not require harm to others
- [ ] Consent considerations addressed
- [ ] Goal serves genuine well-being (yours and others')

**Practical Safety:**
- [ ] Goal is realistic given prerequisites
- [ ] Timeline is reasonable
- [ ] Supporting actions identified
- [ ] Failure scenario is survivable

**Protection Safety:**
- [ ] Daily shield is active
- [ ] Space has been cleared (if operating at home)
- [ ] Protection protocols for this operation type in place
- [ ] Emergency banishing protocol known

## Post-Operation Safety Check

- [ ] Grounded back to normal consciousness
- [ ] Physical body checked (no unusual symptoms)
- [ ] Emotional state checked (processed what arose)
- [ ] Protection reinforced
- [ ] Next steps clear

## Warning Signs to Stop

**Stop operating immediately if:**
- Physical pain, nausea, or dizziness during operation
- Severe emotional disturbance that doesn't settle
- Sense of hostile presence
- Feeling of "something going wrong"
- Losing sense of self or reality

**After stopping:**
- Emergency grounding (physical contact with earth, cold water, physical movement)
- Protection reinforcement
- Do not operate again until cause is understood
- Seek support if symptoms persist

# APPENDIX E: Recommended Practice Schedule

## Beginner (Months 1-3)

**Daily:**
- Morning shield (5 minutes)
- Evening shield reinforcement (3 minutes)
- Basic body scan (5 minutes)

**Weekly:**
- One MVR session on a single chosen goal (30 minutes)
- One diagnostic exercise (15 minutes)
- Log review and reflection (15 minutes)

**Monthly:**
- Space clearing
- Goal assessment and adjustment
- Progress review

## Intermediate (Months 4-12)

**Daily:**
- Morning practice: Shield + MVR + Intention (15-20 minutes)
- Evening practice: Review + Shield reinforcement (10 minutes)

**Weekly:**
- Two MVR sessions (different goals if appropriate)
- One timing-aligned operation (Thursday Jupiter, Friday Venus, etc.)
- ACBE diagnostic on any stuck areas
- Confirmation tracking review

**Monthly:**
- Deep space clearing
- Comprehensive prerequisite assessment
- Operation effectiveness review
- Course correction planning

## Advanced (Year 2+)

**Daily:**
- Integrated morning practice (30-45 minutes)
- Throughout day: Micro-operations as appropriate
- Evening integration practice (15-20 minutes)

**Weekly:**
- Multiple coordinated operations
- Advanced timing stacking
- Teaching or sharing practice (accelerates integration)

**Quarterly:**
- Deep retreat or intensive practice period
- Full system review
- Major goal reassessment

**Yearly:**
- Extended practice (Forty Days or equivalent)
- Complete prerequisite overhaul
- Teaching cycle (if appropriate)
