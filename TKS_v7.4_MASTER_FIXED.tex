\section*{Version 7.4 as Canonical Baseline}

In brief, the reader may treat \textbf{Version~7.4 as the canonical baseline} of TKS. Part~0 introduces the core ontology and algebraic structures (Worlds, Elements, Noetics, Foundations, and Acquisitions), together with the RPM monad and finite Noetic Fractals that form the \emph{Core Calculus}. Subsequent parts develop four major expansion tracks:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Mathematics Track}: Advanced transfinite and spectral fractal theory,
    \item \textbf{Semantics Track}: Enriched domain-theoretic and denotational foundations,
    \item \textbf{Higher Categories Track}: $(\infty,1)$-categorical and computational structure,
    \item \textbf{Geometry Track}: Differential geometric and topological models of Noetic space.
\end{enumerate}
A technically oriented reader may safely begin with the Core Calculus chapter (Chapter~\ref{ch:core-calculus}) and then consult the expansion tracks according to interest.
