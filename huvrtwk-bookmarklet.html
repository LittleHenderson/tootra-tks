Features

  • Floating pill button -- drag to reposition, tap to open controls
  • Glassmorphism control panel -- voice, speed, pitch, delay
  • Desktop: hover over text for 1.5s to hear it read aloud
  • Phone: hold + draw a circle around text to hear it
  • Karaoke mode -- words highlight as they are spoken
  • Remembers your voice and settings between sessions
  • Works on any website -- articles, emails, documents
  • Haptic feedback on mobile when circle is recognized
  • Zero dependencies -- uses built-in Web Speech API
Note: HuvrTwk uses your browser's built-in text-to-speech engine. Available voices depend on your OS. On mobile, you may need to install voice packs in your device's Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-Speech.
